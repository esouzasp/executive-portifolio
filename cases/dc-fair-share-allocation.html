Supply Chain & Planning · Riachuelo

DC Fair Share Allocation

Simplified the phasing model to accelerate fair-share allocation in the DRP and improve product availability at DCs.

Situation
The DRP distributes inventory from the network to Riachuelo's 3 distribution centers, which serve more than 430 stores.
Task
The DRP lacked a fair-share model for the DCs, so allocation could become unbalanced and implementing a full model would take long.
Action
Proposed a simplified modeling approach for phasing, so the fair-share logic could be implemented faster within the DRP, working with the planning and IT teams.
Result
  • Faster implementation of the fair-share logic in the DRP
  • Increased product availability at the DCs
  • Simpler phasing model that planners can follow
