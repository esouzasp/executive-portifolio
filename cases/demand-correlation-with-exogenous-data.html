Demand Correlation with Exogenous Data

Explained demand peaks with exogenous factors, finding strong lagged correlation with Asian supplier market data.

Lag 6correlation, Asian suppliers

Situation
A large animal health company faced demand peaks that affected inventory and service levels.
Task
The peaks looked unpredictable with the internal history alone, so the forecast could not anticipate them.
Action
Ran a study to uncover exogenous factors behind the peaks, correlating stock market data — including data from Asian suppliers — with demand, to use as leading signals in the forecast.
Result
  • High correlation found with a 6-period lag on Asian suppliers
  • Exogenous leading signals available to improve the forecast
  • Explanation for peaks previously seen as unpredictable
