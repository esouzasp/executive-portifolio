Industrial Process Management

Applied quality management tools across industrial business units: BPM, PDCA/SDCA, Six Sigma, Kaizen and process mapping.

Situation
Ypê's industrial business units needed standardized, continuously improving processes and stronger leadership routines.
Task
Processes and management routines were not standardized across the units.
Action
As Industrial Process Management Analyst, applied quality management tools — BPM, PMO, PDCA/SDCA, GROT, Six Sigma, Kaizen and process mapping — and trained managers, supervisors and leaders.
Result
  • Quality management tools applied in industrial BUs
  • Managers, supervisors and leaders trained
  • Process mapping as the basis for standard routines
