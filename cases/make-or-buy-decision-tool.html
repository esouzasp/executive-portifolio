Make or Buy Decision Tool

Led design, implementation and evolution of a deterministic tool to support make-or-buy decisions.

Situation
Riachuelo produces part of its assortment in its own manufacturing complex and sources the rest from a base of more than 400 suppliers.
Task
Deciding what to make in-house and what to buy needed a structured, repeatable method instead of case-by-case analysis.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team in the implementation and evolution of a deterministic make-or-buy model, giving manufacturing and sourcing a common basis to compare options.
Result
  • Deterministic make-or-buy tool in use and evolving
  • Common basis to compare in-house and outsourced production
  • Repeatable method for sourcing decisions
