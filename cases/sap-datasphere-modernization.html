SAP Datasphere Modernization

Rebuilt SAP ECC/S/4 data acquisition with SAP Datasphere and Databricks, cutting the lead time for new integrations from 4 weeks to 2 days.

4 wks → 2 daysintegration lead time

Situation
Supply chain and manufacturing decisions depended on SAP ECC and S/4 data, much of which was missing from the corporate data lake — a large data gap for supply chain and industry.
Task
Every new SAP integration took too long and relied on custom ABAP development, creating cost, dependency and a delivery bottleneck for new data domains.
Action
Implemented SAP Datasphere as the governed acquisition layer for SAP data feeding the Databricks lakehouse. Started with a supply chain pilot of about 50 SAP objects to prove value, then extended the pattern to commercial, manufacturing and logistics.
Result
  • Integration lead time cut from 4 weeks to 2 days
  • Pilot with about 50 SAP objects, extended from supply chain to commercial, manufacturing and logistics
  • Lower ABAP dependency: 600K in the pilot, with a 2M cost reduction potential
  • Reusable pattern for new data domains
