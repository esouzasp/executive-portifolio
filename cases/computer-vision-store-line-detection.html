Computer Vision Store Line Detection

Reviewed the data pipeline for AI computer vision that detects lines in stores, and defined how to scale it.

Situation
Riachuelo uses AI computer vision to detect customer lines in its stores. The detection data needs to reach the analytics platform to be used by store operations.
Task
The original pipeline project for ingesting the computer vision data had gaps, and it was not clear what should be processed at the edge and what on the platform.
Action
Reviewed the pipeline project and its gaps with the data engineering team and the solution partners, and led the trade-off decision on what to process and where, as the basis for scaling the solution.
Result
  • Plan to scale the solution with data engineering
  • Clear trade-off decision on what to process and where
  • Store line data path defined to reach the analytics platform
