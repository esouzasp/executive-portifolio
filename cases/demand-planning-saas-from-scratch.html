Situation
Companies needed modern, accessible demand planning tools, and Planus saw the opportunity to offer demand planning as a SaaS product.
Task
Build a SaaS from scratch that combined statistical depth with a visual, easy-to-use experience for planners.
Action
Led the technical team that built the demand planning SaaS on Azure, with automatic best-fit model selection and a visual interface for planners to review and collaborate on the forecast.
Result
  • Visual and statistical tool to take planning to the next level
  • Best-fit forecasting across about 300 models
  • SaaS product offered to Planus clients
