Transportation Budget & Cost Reduction

Situation
Transportation was one of Ypê's largest logistics costs, with a company-wide budget of about $100M.
Task
The budget needed to reflect occupancy, drop size and cost reduction opportunities, based on real freight data.
Action
As Transportation Projects Analyst, extracted and analyzed the GKO freight databases to build the transportation budget, the occupancy and drop-size targets and the cost reduction breakdown.
Result
  • Budget built for ~$100M of transportation
  • Occupancy, drop size and cost reduction analysis
  • Budget grounded in freight system data
