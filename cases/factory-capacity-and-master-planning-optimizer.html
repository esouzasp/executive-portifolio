Factory Capacity & Master Planning Optimizer

Led design, implementation and evolution of a Python heuristics optimization tool for factory capacity and master planning.

Situation
The manufacturing complex produces over 40M units a year. Factory capacity and the master production plan must balance demand, production lines and constraints.
Task
Planning capacity and the master plan at this scale required an optimization tool that could keep evolving with the business, fed by reliable data.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team in the implementation and evolution of a Python heuristics optimization tool for factory capacity and master planning, including the data engineering that feeds the capacity planning tools.
Result
  • Python heuristics optimization tool in use and evolving
  • Capacity and master planning supported by optimization
  • Capacity planning tools fed by the data platform
