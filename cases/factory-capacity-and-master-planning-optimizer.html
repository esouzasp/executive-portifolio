SmartPUP — Master Production Scheduling

Led the implementation of SmartPUP, a supply chain integrated planning tool for master production scheduling across about 3,000 sewing cells, built on Python heuristics.

3,000sewing cells scheduled

Situation
The manufacturing complex produces over 40M units a year. Factory capacity and the master production plan must balance demand, production lines and constraints.
Task
Scheduling production across about 3,000 sewing cells required an optimization tool that could keep evolving with the business, fed by reliable data.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the implementation and evolution of SmartPUP, a Python heuristics optimization tool for master production scheduling focused on the sewing cells, together with the data engineering that feeds it.
Result
  • Master production scheduling across about 3,000 sewing cells
  • Python heuristics optimization tool in use and evolving
  • Part of supply chain integrated planning, alongside SmartCapacity
