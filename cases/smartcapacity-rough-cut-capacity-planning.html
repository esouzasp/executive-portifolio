SmartCapacity — Rough-Cut Capacity Planning

Led SmartCapacity, a supply chain integrated planning tool to plan and size rough-cut capacity in the medium term.

Situation
The manufacturing complex produces over 40M units a year, and integrated planning needs to know in advance whether capacity can support the plan.
Task
Medium-term capacity had to be planned and sized against the supply plan before detailed scheduling, which required a dedicated tool.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led SmartCapacity, a supply chain integration planning tool for medium-term rough-cut capacity planning and sizing, fed by the data platform and connected to master scheduling in SmartPUP.
Result
  • Medium-term rough-cut capacity planned and sized in one tool
  • Capacity checked against the plan before detailed scheduling
  • Part of supply chain integrated planning, alongside SmartPUP
