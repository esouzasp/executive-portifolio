Statistical Forecasting Models Library

Predictive models in Python (Darts, Orbit, sktime, statsmodels, Prophet, GLM) to improve forecast accuracy across segments.

6segments served

Situation
Clients in agribusiness, animal pharma, food & beverage, food grains, garden products and ice cream needed better demand forecasts for their S&OP.
Task
Demand patterns differed widely across segments — seasonality, weather, market drivers — so no single method fit every client.
Action
Built a reusable set of statistical and machine learning forecasting models in Python (Darts, Orbit, sktime, statsmodels, Prophet, GLM with scikit-learn), following CRISP-DM and integrating the forecasts into the clients' S&OP cycles.
Result
  • Forecast models applied across six segments
  • Reusable approach integrated into S&OP cycles
  • Method selection adapted to each demand pattern
