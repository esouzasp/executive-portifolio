Last-Mile Delay Prediction

Led a data science team on a multi-hub predictive model for last-mile delays across 40K orders/day, avoiding 15% of delays in the pilot.

−15%delays avoided (pilot)

Situation
Last-mile performance drives customer satisfaction in e-commerce. The operation handled around 40K orders a day across multiple hubs.
Task
Last-mile delay rates were high, and delays were only discovered after they happened.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the data science team to develop a simple multi-hub predictive model to anticipate delays, and a report to support the customer support team, starting with a pilot.
Result
  • 15% of delays avoided in the pilot
  • Multi-hub model running over 40K orders per day
  • Report established to support the customer support team
