E-commerce Data Lake Redesign

Redesigned the e-commerce data lake with batch and streaming data, enabling a centralized control tower.

Situation
E-commerce operations depend on integrated commercial, supply and logistics data to build a customer journey that connects e-commerce and operations.
Task
The SAP HANA environment lacked data variety and quality, and the eStore pipeline ran as ETL, limiting new uses such as a control tower.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the e-commerce, supply and logistics data workstreams, redesigning the data lake with batch and streaming data and the eStore pipeline from ETL to ELT on SAP HANA.
Result
  • Data lake delivered with batch and streaming data
  • eStore pipeline redesigned from ETL to ELT on SAP HANA
  • Foundation for the centralized control tower project
