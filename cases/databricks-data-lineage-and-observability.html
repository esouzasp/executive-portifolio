Data Lineage & Pipeline Monitoring

Led a data engineering product that maps the lineage of 110 strategic reports in Databricks and monitors more than 2,000 pipeline flows in real time.

2,000+pipelines monitored in real time

Situation
With more than 50 strategic projects and data products running over retail operations, the Databricks lakehouse kept growing in pipelines, tables and consumers.
Task
Without lineage, it was hard to see how data flowed between pipelines and products, to optimize processing and to trace the impact of a failure.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team that built a data engineering product for lineage and monitoring: lineage mapping of 110 strategic reports in Databricks and real-time monitoring of more than 2,000 pipeline flows.
Result
  • Lineage mapped for 110 strategic reports
  • 2,000+ pipeline flows monitored in real time
  • Basis to optimize pipelines and trace failure impact
