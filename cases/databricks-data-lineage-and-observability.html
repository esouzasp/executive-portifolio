Databricks Data Lineage & Observability

Led design and implementation of data lineage mapping in Databricks to optimize pipelines and enable observability.

Situation
With more than 50 strategic projects and data products running over retail operations, the Databricks lakehouse kept growing in pipelines, tables and consumers.
Task
Without lineage, it was hard to see how data flowed between pipelines and products, to optimize processing and to trace the impact of a failure.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team that mapped data lineage across the Databricks pipelines, as part of the data governance developments, to support pipeline optimization and observability.
Result
  • Data lineage mapped across Databricks pipelines
  • Basis to optimize pipelines and trace failure impact
  • Observability added to the data governance toolset
