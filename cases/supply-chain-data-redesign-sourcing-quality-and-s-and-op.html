Supply Chain Data Redesign — Sourcing, Quality & S&OP

Led design and implementation of the supply chain data redesign covering sourcing, quality and S&OP/S&OE.

Situation
With more than 400 suppliers and over 3,000 new products a month, sourcing, quality and S&OP/S&OE decisions depend on integrated supply chain data.
Task
Supply chain data was fragmented across processes and systems, so each area worked with its own partial view.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team that redesigned and integrated the supply chain data for sourcing, quality and S&OP/S&OE on the data platform.
Result
  • Sourcing, quality and S&OP/S&OE data redesigned and integrated
  • Single data foundation for supply chain decisions
  • S&OP and S&OE supported by the same data
