End-to-End Order Monitoring with ElasticSearch

End-to-end order management monitoring with ElasticSearch logs, metrics and tracing.

Situation
Orders flow across SAP, Cello TMS, the VTEX e-commerce and the marketplaces, with teams and stakeholders in Brazil and Korea.
Task
When an integration failed somewhere along the chain, it was hard to detect where and why, which affected order status and the customer journey.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the implementation of end-to-end order management monitoring with ElasticSearch — logs, metrics and tracing — working with the indirect teams of data engineering, TMS and SAP.
Result
  • Faster detection of integration failures
  • Failures traceable across SAP, TMS, VTEX and marketplaces, helping teams resolve them
  • End-to-end visibility of the order flow
