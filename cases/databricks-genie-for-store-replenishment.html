Databricks Genie Pilot for Store Replenishment

Led a pilot of Databricks Genie on replenishment data so the operations team can answer questions from 400+ stores in natural language.

400+stores served by the operations team

Situation
The operations team supports more than 400 stores, which raise recurring questions about replenishment.
Task
Answering each store meant searching reports and data by hand, which limited how much the operations team could interact with the stores.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the design and the engineering team for a pilot that set up Databricks Genie on governed replenishment data, with business context and curated instructions, so the operations team can ask questions in natural language.
Result
  • Pilot: operations team answers replenishment questions from 400+ stores in natural language
  • Answers grounded in governed replenishment data
  • Broader interaction between the operations team and the stores
