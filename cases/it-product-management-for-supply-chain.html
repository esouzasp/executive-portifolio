IT Product Management for Supply Chain

Raised product management maturity in supply chain technology with clear roles and governance rituals.

Situation
Supply chain and manufacturing technology demands compete for the same IT capacity, budget and partners, with many stakeholders across sourcing, planning, quality and operations.
Task
Product management maturity was low: weak prioritization, little follow-up of the backlog and no shared routines between business and IT.
Action
Reorganized the process with defined roles for GPM, PM and PMO, structured technology demand management and prioritization, and established governance routines with supply chain leadership and IT teams.
Result
  • Tech and business rituals to follow up the backlog
  • Better prioritization of high-impact projects
  • Technology governance routines with supply chain leadership and IT
  • 20+ strategic and tactical projects delivered
