o9 IBP & Capacity Planning Data Engineering

Leading data engineering for the o9 Integrated Business Planning (IBP) platform — about 40 integrations — and for the capacity planning tools.

~40integrations for o9 IBP

Situation
Riachuelo adopted o9 for integrated business planning (IBP), connecting commercial, supply chain and manufacturing plans, alongside dedicated capacity planning tools.
Task
IBP and capacity planning are only as good as the data that feeds them: reliable, timely flows from the corporate systems were required.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, leads the data engineering design and the delivery of about 40 integrations that feed o9 IBP and the capacity planning tools from the data platform, organized in two go-live phases.
Result
  • About 40 integrations designed for o9 IBP
  • Go-live planned: phase 1 in January 2027, phase 2 by the end of 2027
  • Capacity planning tools fed by the data platform
