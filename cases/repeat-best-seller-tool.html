Repeat Best Seller Tool

Defined requirements for best seller and FR tracking, delivered as a Databricks model and an order tracking system.

Situation
In fashion retail, best sellers and FR items concentrate a relevant share of sales, and repeating them at the right time drives availability and results.
Task
Tracking and prioritizing best seller (BS) and FR projects was difficult, with no systematic way to identify repeat best sellers or follow their orders.
Action
Led the construction of the solution requirements with the business and technology teams, defining both the identification of repeat best sellers and the tracking of their orders.
Result
  • Best seller identification model in Databricks
  • Order tracking system for repeat best sellers and FR
  • Clear requirements shared by business and technology
