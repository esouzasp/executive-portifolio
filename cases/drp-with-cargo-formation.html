DRP with Cargo Formation

Multimodal DRP model (road, rail, cabotage) that let one planner do the work of three in less time.

3 → 1planners, in less time

Situation
At Ypê, transfers between plants and distribution centers could use road, rail or cabotage, each with different cost, lead time and load rules.
Task
The DRP model ran manually in Excel and required three planners to build the transfer plan.
Action
As Supply Planner, modeled the road, rail and cabotage transfer modes with cargo formation inside the DRP, automating the build of the transfer plan.
Result
  • From 3 planners to 1, in less time
  • Transfer plan covering road, rail and cabotage
  • Cargo formation built into the DRP
