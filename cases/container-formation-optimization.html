Container Formation Optimization

Optimization tool that cut container formation for order taking from 4 days to 30 minutes.

4 days → 30 mincontainer planning

Situation
A mid-size home décor retailer buys imported products, and order taking requires grouping items into containers.
Task
Container formation for each order cycle took 4 days of manual work, delaying purchase decisions.
Action
Modeled an optimization tool in Excel + Solver that forms the containers automatically, respecting the order constraints.
Result
  • Tool delivered to plan containers
  • Process reduced from 4 days to 30 minutes
  • Planners freed for analysis instead of manual grouping
