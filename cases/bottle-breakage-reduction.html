Bottle Breakage Reduction

Improvement group that helped reduce bottle breakage in beer production by about 500K per year.

~500K/yrbottle breakage reduced

Situation
The career started at AmBev at age 16 through an apprenticeship, moving on to production operator and logistics operations. Returnable assets — bottles, crates, pallets — are critical in beverage operations.
Task
Bottle breakage in beer production created losses, and asset and production control relied on manual spreadsheets.
Action
Controlled the inventory of returnable assets for wholesalers and internal DCs, production records and KPIs, built Visual Basic automations for operations and production planning, and took part in the results improvement group.
Result
  • Bottle breakage reduced by about 500K per year
  • Spreadsheet automations for operations and production planning (PPCP)
