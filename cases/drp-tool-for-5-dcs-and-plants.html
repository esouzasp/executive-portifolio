DRP Tool for 5 DCs and Plants

Excel + VBA planning tool for 5 DCs and plants that captured cabotage savings and cut rail by 30%.

1M/yrsavings with cabotage

Situation
Ypê distributes from its plants through 5 distribution centers in Brazil; distribution planning drove transport mode decisions.
Task
Planning and control of transportation and transfers was largely manual, in spreadsheets, limiting the use of cheaper modes.
Action
As Supply Planner, developed an Excel + VBA tool to automate the planning and control of transfers across the 5 DCs and plants, and started a cabotage project.
Result
  • Savings of 1M per year with cabotage
  • Rail transport −30%
  • Transfer planning and control automated
