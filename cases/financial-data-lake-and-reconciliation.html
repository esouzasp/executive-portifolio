Financial Data Lake & Reconciliation

Built the data ingestion and model that took financial reconciliation out of Excel.

~60Mreconciliation mitigated

Situation
D2C operations between e-commerce and last mile generated high volumes of financial transactions across systems that had to be reconciled.
Task
Financial reconciliation was done manually in Excel over large data volumes, leaving a large amount of open items and instability in D2C operations.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the data ingestion and the data model that took reconciliation out of Excel and into the BI database, speeding up the process.
Result
  • ~60M in financial reconciliation mitigated, stabilizing D2C operations between e-commerce and last mile
  • 5M records of movements in the BI database
  • Open items reduced from 20K (60M) to about 5K
