Manufacturing & Operations · Riachuelo

Factory FP&A, Cost & Margin Engine

Led design and implementation of an engine that consolidates factory inventory, sales and cost data to manage product cost and margin.

Situation
Riachuelo runs an end-to-end fashion value chain with its own manufacturing complex producing over 40M units a year. Margin decisions in manufacturing depend on combining factory inventory, sales and production cost data that lived in different sources.
Task
Calculating cost and margin for factory inventory and sales was manual and inconsistent across areas, which weakened FP&A and the cost management of manufacturing.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team that built a cost and margin management engine: integrating inventory, sales and cost data on the data platform, defining one calculation logic for cost and margin, and making it available to finance, manufacturing and commercial teams.
Result
  • FP&A and cost management for manufacturing developed
  • One engine for factory inventory, sales, cost and margin
  • Consistent margin view supporting manufacturing and commercial decisions
