Supply Chain Digital Roadmap

Built the first technology roadmap for supply chain, centralizing demands into a prioritized portfolio.

35projects · 25M budget

Situation
Reporting to the VP of Supply Chain and supporting the CDO and CTO, the role covered digital transformation across sourcing, integrated planning, foreign trade, quality, product development, manufacturing and operations.
Task
Supply chain areas raised technology needs through scattered channels, and there was no roadmap to guide investment, priorities and the work of IT teams and partners.
Action
Ran a discovery with the business areas, centralized all technology demands in a single portfolio, supported business cases and investment assessments, and built a 3-year strategic roadmap for supply chain technology and data.
Result
  • 3-year strategic roadmap with 35+ initiatives
  • 25M budget organized by priority
  • Single portfolio for supply chain technology demands
