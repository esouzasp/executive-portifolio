E-commerce D2C Order Integration

Led a cross-team effort that integrated D2C orders with SCM and TMS, cutting order status errors by 90%.

−90%order status errors

Situation
In 2023, Samsung's D2C operation handled around 40K orders a day through its eStore and marketplaces such as Mercado Livre, Amazon, Fast Shop and Magazine Luiza, flowing through SAP, TMS and the last mile.
Task
D2C lacked integration with SCM and TMS. Order status errors concentrated at the marketplace entry point, where they reached up to 90%, affecting the customer experience.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led a cross-team group — data engineering, TMS, SAP and e-commerce — to redesign the order management integration between SAP, Cello TMS, the VTEX e-commerce and the marketplaces, supported by end-to-end monitoring.
Result
  • 90% reduction in order status errors end to end at the marketplaces
  • Order management integration redesigned across SAP, TMS, VTEX and marketplaces
  • Stabler D2C operation between e-commerce and last mile
