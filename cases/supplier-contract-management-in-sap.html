Supplier Contract Management in SAP

Moved external production supplier management from Excel to SAP standard contracts, keeping the SAP core clean.

Situation
Part of Riachuelo's production is outsourced to external suppliers, which requires control of terms, volumes and supplier commitments within a base of more than 400 suppliers.
Task
Management of external production suppliers ran on Excel spreadsheets, with low control and traceability, outside the SAP core.
Action
As Senior Manager of Supply Chain Data & Technology, bridging the business and IT, led the discovery of SAP standard modules to find a native solution instead of custom development, and translated the supplier management needs into requirements for SAP contracts.
Result
  • Solution kept within the SAP core, using contracts to manage external production
  • Custom development avoided by adopting standard functionality
  • Supplier management moved out of spreadsheets
