Demand Collaboration Tool on Databricks

Led design and implementation of a demand collaboration tool where planners work in embedded Excel integrated with Databricks.

Situation
With more than 360K SKUs and over 3,000 new products a month, demand planning depends on collaboration between planners — and planners work in Excel.
Task
Collaboration inputs lived in disconnected spreadsheets outside the data platform, with no single version, governance or link to the planning data.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team that delivered a demand collaboration tool with an embedded Excel front end integrated with Databricks: planners keep a familiar interface while inputs are read from and written to the platform.
Result
  • Planners collaborate in a familiar Excel interface
  • Collaboration inputs stored and governed in Databricks
  • Demand inputs connected to the planning data
