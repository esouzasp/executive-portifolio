Refrigerator Returns Root Cause

Traced a sudden spike in refrigerator returns to a procedure change at the Mexico plant using CRM data.

Situation
Returns hit cost, customer experience and brand. Refrigerator returns were tracked as part of last-mile carrier performance management.
Task
The refrigerator return rate rose suddenly, and logistics data alone did not explain why.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the data analysis to find the root cause, combining returns, logistics and CRM data, and took the findings to the operations and manufacturing stakeholders.
Result
  • CRM data revealed a recurring defect (hole) on the top of the units
  • Root cause traced to a procedure change at the Mexico plant
  • Issue taken from logistics to manufacturing with data evidence
