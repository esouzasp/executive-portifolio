Logistics Cost Data Lakehouse

Logistics cost lakehouse enabling better freight analytics and simulations.

Situation
Freight is one of the main cost drivers of e-commerce and last-mile operations, across many carriers and hubs.
Task
Freight cost data was not organized for analysis or simulation, which limited decisions on carriers and logistics costs.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the logistics cost data lakehouse and the freight analytics and simulations built on top of it.
Result
  • Freight cost analytics on a single lakehouse
  • Freight simulations to support logistics decisions
  • Cost data feeding carrier performance management
