Oil & Gas Inventory Optimization

Web optimization tool for serialized inventory that cut replanning from 4 days to minutes.

4 days → mininventory replanning

Situation
An oil and gas operation manages high-value, serialized inventory across many locations, where each item must be tracked and allocated individually.
Task
Replanning inventory and allocation took about 4 days, too slow for the operation's needs.
Action
Led the development of a web application with an optimization engine for serialized, item-level inventory management and allocation.
Result
  • Serialized, item-level inventory management
  • Replanning reduced from 4 days to a few minutes
  • Web tool usable by the operation without spreadsheets
