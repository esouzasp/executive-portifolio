Large-Volume EDI Platform for Key Retail Accounts

As Product Owner, led a large-volume EDI platform connecting sell-out and inventory data from Brazil's main key accounts, built in-house on the Apache big data stack.

Situation
Samsung SDS, Samsung's IT arm, supported Samsung's supply chain, S&OP/IBP and CPFR processes in Brazil. Samsung needed sell-out and inventory visibility from its main Brazilian retail key accounts, exchanged electronically (EDI) in large volumes.
Task
The sell-out data collection ran on a market tool that had to be migrated, and each key account had its own data requirements to implement and support.
Action
As Product Owner, led the migration from the market tool and the stabilization of an in-house big data platform, and a technical team of about 8 people that supported the key accounts and implemented their requirements. The framework used Apache Hadoop and Hive, an Oracle database, Apache NiFi for data flows, Apache Ambari for process monitoring and Kylo for job management.
Result
  • Sell-out and inventory connected across Brazil's main key accounts
  • Platform running within a few months and internalized by Samsung
  • In-house platform replacing market solutions
  • Customer requirements implemented with dedicated technical support
