SaaS Go-to-Market on a Lean Budget

Designed a lean sales model with a fixed-plus-success SDR contract, closing 5 clients in 6 months.

5clients in 6 months

Situation
As a startup, Planus needed to grow revenue for its demand planning SaaS without a large sales team.
Task
The service had to be sold with a limited budget while keeping operating costs under control.
Action
Designed a lean go-to-market: contracted an SDR on a fixed fee plus closing-based compensation, aligning sales cost with results.
Result
  • 5 clients closed in 6 months
  • Operating costs sustained
  • Sales cost tied to closed deals
