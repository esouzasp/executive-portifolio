Supply Chain Sourcing Cross Quotation (RFQ)

RFQ tool connecting the supply chain team with 500+ external suppliers to expand the supply base, with a full ROI potential of R$18M a year.

R$18M/yrfull ROI potential

Situation
Riachuelo sources from a base of more than 400 suppliers and set out to expand its supply network.
Task
Running quotations with external suppliers was hard to scale and compare, limiting the expansion of the supply base.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the solution for a cross-quotation (RFQ) tool that connects the supply chain team with external suppliers and structures quotations so they can be compared across suppliers.
Result
  • Supply chain team connected with 500+ external suppliers
  • Supply base expansion supported by structured quotations
  • Full ROI potential of R$18M per year
