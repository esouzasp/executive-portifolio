Address-Not-Found Analysis

Uncovered that 30% of carriers were misreporting 'address not found', giving logistics visibility to act on the real causes.

30%carriers misreporting the reason

Situation
Failed deliveries create cost, repeated attempts and a poor customer experience. Delivery fail reasons were part of last-mile carrier performance management.
Task
The rate of 'address not found' in the last mile was high, and the reason reported by carriers was taken at face value.
Action
As Manager of Supply Chain Projects & Data Analytics, bridging business requirements with the data and development teams, led the analysis of delivery fail reasons across carriers to understand the real root causes.
Result
  • 30% of carriers were reporting a false reason
  • Logistics gained visibility to act on the real causes
  • Carrier performance management based on evidence
