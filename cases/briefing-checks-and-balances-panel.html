Briefing Checks & Balances Panel

Led design and implementation of the briefing data integration and a BI panel for checks and balances reporting.

Situation
Product briefings start each collection and drive commercial and supply decisions for over 3,000 new products a month. What was briefed must be checked against what is executed.
Task
Briefing data was not integrated with the rest of the commercial data, so checks and balances between plan and execution were hard to report.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the architecture design and the engineering team that integrated the briefing tool data into the data platform and delivered a business intelligence panel for checks and balances reporting, as part of the commercial data redesign.
Result
  • Briefing data integrated into the data platform
  • BI panel for checks and balances between briefing and execution
  • Part of the commercial data redesign (briefing, margin, OTB)
