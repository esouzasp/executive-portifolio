Fashion & Commercial Briefing Checks & Balances

Led the review of the solution architecture and the rebuild of the tool that manages and tracks how fashion and commercial briefings evolve, with checks and balances reporting.

Situation
Product briefings start each collection and drive commercial and supply decisions for over 3,000 new products a month. What was briefed must be checked against what is executed.
Task
The existing briefing tool and its data were not integrated with the rest of the commercial data, so following how briefings evolved — and checking plan against execution — was hard.
Action
As Senior Manager of Enterprise Data & Analytics, led the review of the solution architecture and the rebuild of the tool used to manage and track the evolution of fashion and commercial briefings, integrating briefing data into the data platform and delivering a BI panel for checks and balances.
Result
  • Solution architecture reviewed and briefing tool rebuilt
  • Evolution of fashion and commercial briefings managed and tracked in one tool
  • Briefing data integrated, with a BI panel for checks and balances
